Engineer's calculators

3-phase motor full-load current

—

I = P÷(√3·V·cosφ·η). Breaker suggestion = 1.25× FLA (round up to next standard size).

Pneumatic cylinder force

—

Push = p·A(bore); pull = p·A(bore−rod). 1 bar = 100 kPa.

4–20 mA loop headroom

—

Worst case at 20 mA: drop = 0.02·R. Healthy loop keeps ≥ transmitter minimum across the receiver.
