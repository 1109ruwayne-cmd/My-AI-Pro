Description (~150 words)
Smash AI Pro™ is a complete AI studio that runs in the browser and ships as a signed Android app and native Windows builds. At its core: multi-model chat routing across 7 AI providers with automatic federation fallback, backed by a persistent knowledge base and an owner dashboard. BrandForge generates full brand identity bibles in about 90 seconds — manifesto, taglines, pillars, SVG logos, colour palettes, typography, voice rules and applications — with a compliance auditor and SVG/JSON/Markdown export. Creative Studio adds three production engines: DesignForge renders template-based social graphics, QR codes and icon packs server-side; VideoForge performs real ffmpeg editing (trim, speed, resize presets, captions, GIF conversion, audio extraction) through async jobs; 3DForge builds live 3D text in the browser with gold/chrome/neon materials, turntable WebM recording, PNG snapshots and GLB export for Blender or Unity. A South African certification registry, professional desks and tiered billing complete the studio. Built by a solo maker in Gqeberha, South Africa — self-hosted, store-independent by design.
