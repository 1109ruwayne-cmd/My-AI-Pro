# Credit health in South Africa - your rights, free reports, disputes

My AI Pro's SA Trust Desk: guidance, letters and public-register checks for
credit matters. Defensive and lawful only - My AI Pro never pulls anyone's
bureau record without a bureau/reseller agreement, a permissible purpose under
the National Credit Act and the data subject's POPIA consent. It helps people
use THEIR OWN rights instead.

## Your free credit reports (National Credit Act s72)

- Every South African gets **one free credit report every 12 months from EACH
  registered bureau** - TransUnion, Experian, Compuscan and XDS. That is four
  free reports a year; no reason needed, no charge. Further copies cost a
  regulated fee (about R20 excl. VAT).
- Request with your SA ID number at the bureau's own site or call centre:
  transunion.co.za · experian.co.za · compuscan.co.za · xds.co.za
  (ClearScore and Splendi give free ongoing views of some bureau data.)
- Checking YOUR OWN report is a **soft enquiry - it never lowers your score**.
  Only credit applications cause hard enquiries.

## Reading a report - what may legally sit there

- **Enquiries and adverse classifications (late payments, defaults):** drop off
  after **1 year**.
- **Judgments:** 5 years, or immediately once paid up / rescinded by court.
- **Paid-up write-offs:** removed under **s71A** once settled - the "paid-up
  judgment/write-off" rule; the bureau must remove them after settlement.
- **Prescribed debt** (generally 3 years old, unacknowledged, no judgment):
  may NOT be listed or collected. If it appears, dispute it as prescribed.
- **Credit life insurance, affordability flags, sequestration/administration
  records:** have their own clocks - ask if unsure.

## Disputing wrong information (s72(3)) - free

1. Get the report showing the error; note bureau, creditor, account no., entry.
2. Lodge a written dispute with the bureau (ID + proof: paid-up letter,
   settlement agreement, rescission order, bank statements). Use the Trust Desk
   letter skill (`trust_desk`, action `dispute_letter`) to draft it.
3. The bureau has **20 business days** (Reg 17(4)) to investigate with the
   source. Under the NCR's credible-evidence guideline (Dec 2025) the disputed
   item should be **masked while investigated**; if the source cannot verify
   it, the bureau **must remove it** and tell you in writing.
4. Unresolved? Escalate free: **National Financial Ombud** (absorbed the Credit
   Ombud) 0861 662 837 / ombud@creditombud.org.za, then the **NCR**
   0860 627 627 / complaints@ncr.org.za (Form 29).

## Is a lender legal? (NCR register)

- Every lawful credit provider (incl. micro-lenders) is registered with the
  **National Credit Regulator** and carries an **NCRCP registration number**.
  Check the register on ncr.org.za before signing anything.
- Red flags: no NCRCP number on adverts/contracts; upfront "initiation" cash
  before credit; interest/fees above NCA caps; no written contract; pressure to
  hand over ID/books/SASSA cards (reckless lending - report it).
- Report unregistered lending to the NCR (0860 627 627) or SAPS for loan-shark
  conduct. Never pay a "credit repair" firm promising to erase valid listings -
  the statutory dispute route above is free and stronger.

## Company & VAT trust checks (public registers)

- **Company status:** CIPC - eservices.cipc.co.za / BizPortal public search:
  registration number, status (Active / In deregistration), directors. Ask the
  seller for a current COR/certificate and match it.
- **VAT vendor:** SARS vendor verification - a genuine VAT number is **10
  digits starting with 4**. The Trust Desk skill validates the format; final
  confirmation via SARS (eFiling vendor search / VAT registration letter).
- **Registered credit provider:** NCR register as above.
These checks are public-register lookups - lawful for anyone, no consent needed
to *look at a register*; consent IS needed to pull a person's bureau file.

## POPIA consent for third-party checks

When a business (landlord, employer, lender) checks a PERSON's credit or
records: purpose must be lawful (NCA permissible purpose), consent must be
informed, specific and recorded, and only the minimum data processed. The Trust
Desk skill (`consent_form`) drafts a POPIA-compliant consent document. My AI Pro
stores consent records in the owner's registry ledger when the owner begins
offering bureau-backed checks under a reseller agreement.
