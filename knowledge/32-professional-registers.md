# 32 - Professional Registration Checks (SA + Australia)

Added 2026-10-28. How to verify that a professional is REALLY registered - free, legal,
public channels only. Rule of the desk: the live register is the only proof. Certificates,
screenshots, email signatures and CV claims can all be forged.

## Project managers / construction managers - SACPCMP (South Africa)

- **Who**: South African Council for the Project & Construction Management Professions.
  STATUTORY body under the Project & Construction Management Professions Act 48 of 2000.
  The only body that can legally register project/construction managers in SA.
- **Free public check**: sacpcmp.org.za → "Registered Person Search" → "Project &
  Construction Management" search facility (search.mymembership.co.za). Separate search
  facilities exist for Construction Health & Safety and Building Inspectors. Live feed from
  the registration platform - shows current status immediately.
- **Categories**: Pr.CM (Professional Construction Manager), Pr.CPM (Professional
  Construction Project Manager), Candidate CM, Candidate CPM, Professional Construction
  Mentor. Pr.CPM is a SAQA-recognised professional designation (pbdesig.saqa.org.za,
  designation ID 498, reregistered to 2030-03-19).
- **Genuine certificate markers** (per SACPCMP Registrar, Engineering News 2022):
  - QR code on the certificate linking to live online validation.
  - Validity follows the SACPCMP financial year: April → end of MARCH.
  - **End date in any month other than March = fake certificate.**
  - Name on certificate must match the online validation profile.
  - Registered persons can generate a validation key + PDF status certificate via the
    self-serve portal (used for tenders/jobs). PrivySeal is the digital verification partner.
- **Registration requirements (rules effective 1 Aug 2025 + limited special dispensation)**:
  - Pr.CPM / Pr.CM: NQF 8 qualification, 480 credits, SAQA-accredited (dispensation accepts
    NQF 7); CHS Agents NQF 7 (disp. 6); CHS Officers SAMTRAC NQF 5; CHS Managers NQF 5
    (disp.); Building Inspectors NQF 7 (disp. 6); NEBOSH Int. Diploma NQF 8 now accepted.
  - 4-10 years logged experience depending on category; annual CPD + annual fee compliance.
  - All qualifications verified through MIE (Managed Integrity Evaluation).
- **How to register (application "forms")**: mypcm.sacpcmp.org.za online portal -
  certified ID copy, certified qualifications, detailed CV, Annexure A1 (project profile)
  + Annexure A2 (project report). Registration certificate valid one financial year,
  renewed on payment of the annual fee.
- **PMSA** (Project Management South Africa, pmsa.org.za): voluntary professional body -
  membership/certification is NOT statutory registration. Don't confuse the two.
- **Foreign PM credentials**: PMP → verify via PMI (pmi.org) / Credly digital badge;
  PRINCE2 → PeopleCert (peoplecert.org) certificate verification. Both are private
  certification schemes, not SA statutory registration.

## Nurses & midwives - SANC (South Africa)

- **Who**: South African Nursing Council, statutory under the Nursing Act 33 of 2005.
- **Free public check**: sanc.co.za/eRegister.aspx → accept conditions → search by SANC
  reference number OR SA ID number. The eRegister is published under s35 of the Act and is
  a legal COPY of the active records of the official register.
- **No hit = not registered.** SANC directive (Circular 8/2024): employers MUST refrain
  from employing nurses without current SANC registration or removed under s44.
- **Electronic APCs**: from July 2025 annual practising certificates are emailed as
  encrypted, password-protected PDFs with a QR code (QR confirms eRegister address,
  reference number, ID number, APC number). From July 2026 printed APCs stop except
  exceptional cases. Employers MUST verify the electronic APC against the eRegister.
- **2026 annual fees (gazetted 13 June 2025, indicative)**: R870 registered nurse/midwife,
  R520 enrolled nurse/midwife, R370 enrolled nursing auxiliary; reduced fees for 60+.
  Employers normally pay on behalf of practitioners (Resolution 3 of 2019).
  Restoration of lapsed registration: restoration fee + ALL arrears.
- **Official written verification**: SANC fee list includes VERIFEE (verification fees) and
  EXRFEE (extract from register) - for when a paper verification letter is formally needed
  (e.g. emigration). The free eRegister check suffices for employment screening.

## Nurses & health practitioners - NMBA / AHPRA (Australia)

- **Who**: Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia, administered via AHPRA under the
  National Registration & Accreditation Scheme. Covers nursing, midwifery + 14 other
  regulated professions (medical, dental, pharmacy, psychology, paramedicine, etc.).
- **Free public check**: ahpra.gov.au → "Look up a practitioner" (Registers of
  Practitioners). Search by name or AHPRA registration number. Updated continuously;
  AHPRA: "the only reliable source of information about practitioners' registration status."
- **Read the whole record**: registration status, type/subtype, expiry, principal place of
  practice, and any CONDITIONS, undertakings or endorsements (e.g. nurse practitioner
  endorsement). Disciplinary decision links are published on the record.
- **Not on the register = not registered to practise in Australia.** Old pre-national-scheme
  numbers don't work; registration names may differ from common names (ask for the number).
  Confidential queries: 1300 419 495. There are also published lists of cancelled
  practitioners and practitioners who agreed not to practise.
- **Renewal**: nurses/midwives renew by 31 MAY each year (registration runs 1 June → 31 May);
  late fees apply, lapses after 30 June → removed from register.
- **Don't trust printouts/PDF certificates** - AHPRA explicitly says use the online
  register. Registered practitioners can download their own Certificate of Registration
  from the practitioner portal (~2 hours after approval).
- **SA nurses moving to Australia (IQNM pathway)**: self-check + qualification assessment
  via the IQNM dashboard first, then apply through the AHPRA practitioner portal. Requires
  a certificate of good standing from the home council (SANC for South Africans), certified
  qualification copies (+ translations), criminal history declaration, identity
  verification (doable before arriving), English language evidence where applicable.

## Tax practitioners - SARS (South Africa)

- **Law**: Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011, s240/240A. Anyone who, for consideration,
  advises on the application of a tax Act or completes/assists with another person's
  return MUST register with a Recognised Controlling Body (RCB) AND with SARS - within
  21 business days of first providing the service.
- **Free public check**: SARS eFiling Tax Practitioner Query -
  https://secure.sarsefiling.co.za/TaxPractitionerQuery.aspx ("Find out if your Tax
  Practitioner is registered"). Ask for the PR number (always starts with "PR").
- **Double registration**: valid practitioner = on the SARS register AND a member in good
  standing of an RCB. Statutory RCBs are listed in s240A(1); approved RCBs (SAIT, SAICA,
  ACCA, CIMA, etc.) are listed on sars.gov.za. Cross-check membership on the RCB's own
  site - the two must agree.
- **Registration process**: RCB initiates on SARS eFiling → practitioner completes/updates
  the RAV01 → selects RCB and submits → SARS validates → outcome on Registration Work Page
  (Approved / Request for documents / Pending); PR number issued via eFiling
  correspondence. No disqualifying criminal convictions (s240(3)).
- **Red flags**: no PR number; refuses to name an RCB; upfront cash "refund fixing";
  non-professional email addresses; asks client to sign blank RAV01/ITR12; "registration"
  certificates that don't survive the eFiling query.
- **Misconduct / unregistered practice**: report to SARS -
  Reportingunprofessionalconduct@sars.gov.za (see SARS "Guide to Reporting Unprofessional
  Conduct", updated Jan 2026; SARS re-issued scam warnings July 2026).
- VAT number format check remains a separate first gate (trust_desk sars_vat: 10 digits
  starting with 4; confirm via eFiling/vendor letter).

## Desk rules (lawful lines)

1. Public register lookups are free and legal - no login needed for the checks above.
2. NEVER write to, register on, or submit applications to these systems on anyone's behalf
   without an explicit, recorded mandate from the person concerned.
3. POPIA: searching public registers about a client/applicant for a legitimate screening
   purpose is fine; store only what is needed, record the check date, and don't build
   shadow dossiers.
4. Report forgery the right way: to the body itself (SACPCMP prosecutes fraudulent
   documents to "the fullest extent of the law"), not vigilante action.
5. Cross-check designations on SAQA (pbdesig.saqa.org.za) - if a "professional
   designation" isn't registered there, the title carries no statutory weight.
